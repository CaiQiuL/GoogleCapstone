Tools Used: Tableau, Excel, Python, R

# Introduction
This project explores the relationship between household income and educational outcomes across U.S. states. Using publicly available data, I analyzed the correlation between income levels and high school graduation rates, postsecondary education enrollment, and unemployment rates.

The goal was to answer one key question: “Does income affect graduation rates and educational attainment across the United States?”

# Data Sources
Bureau of Labor Statistics
U.S. Department of Education
National Center for Education Statistics
Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System
12-month Enrollment component final data.

# Conclusion
While average income did not positively correlate with graduation rate, postsecondary education rates do improve average household income.
